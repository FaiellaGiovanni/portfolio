## Overview

In this course laboratory, I study how real second-order systems behave, and how well the textbook models predict them.

## Method

- Each week I collect data on an instrumented spring-mounted cart, varying its mass and stiffness.
- I simulate the governing equations of motion in MATLAB and compare the predicted response with the response measured in the lab.

## What I learned

Comparing measured responses against model predictions built my physical intuition for how second-order ODE systems behave.
